# Target Users and Personas

> Detailed profiles of StayHaven's primary users and their needs

---

## 👥 User Categories

StayHaven serves **four distinct user groups**, each with unique needs and goals:

1. **Hotel Owners** - Business operators
2. **Guests** - Hotel customers
3. **Hotel Staff** - Operational team members
4. **Platform Admins** - System administrators

---

## 🏨 Persona 1: Hotel Owner

### **Meet Rajesh - Independent Hotel Owner**

**Demographics**:
- Age: 42
- Location: Kathmandu, Nepal
- Business: Owns 2 hotels (25 rooms, 15 rooms)
- Tech Savviness: Moderate

**Goals**:
- ✅ Increase direct bookings to reduce OTA commissions
- ✅ Manage both properties from one system
- ✅ Track revenue and occupancy rates
- ✅ Reduce operational costs
- ✅ Improve staff efficiency

**Pain Points**:
- ❌ Pays for 5 different software subscriptions ($600/month)
- ❌ Can't see consolidated data across properties
- ❌ Loses 20% revenue to OTA commissions
- ❌ Manual staff scheduling and communication
- ❌ No visibility into kitchen operations

**How StayHaven Helps**:
- All-in-one platform ($199/month)
- Multi-property dashboard
- 10% commission on platform bookings
- Real-time operational visibility
- Staff management tools

**User Journey**:
1. Signs up and creates company profile
2. Lists both hotels with photos and details
3. Invites managers and staff
4. Tracks bookings and revenue in real-time
5. Makes data-driven decisions

**Key Features Used**:
- Multi-property management
- Revenue analytics
- Staff role assignment
- Hotel listing management
- Booking management

## 👤 Persona 2: Guest/Traveler

### **Meet Priya - Business Traveler**

**Demographics**:
- Age: 28
- Location: Mumbai, India
- Occupation: Marketing Manager
- Travel Frequency: 8-10 trips/year
- Tech Savviness: High

**Goals**:
- ✅ Find hotels quickly with good amenities
- ✅ Book seamlessly without phone calls
- ✅ Order room service easily
- ✅ Get quick responses to requests
- ✅ Track service status

**Pain Points**:
- ❌ Too many OTA options, confusing
- ❌ Hotel websites are often outdated
- ❌ Room service requires phone calls
- ❌ No idea when food will arrive
- ❌ Calling reception for every small request

**How StayHaven Helps**:
- Clean, modern search interface
- Direct hotel booking (better rates)
- In-app room service ordering
- Real-time order tracking with ETAs
- One-tap waiter call button

**User Journey**:
1. Searches for hotels in destination city
2. Filters by price, rating, amenities
3. Views detailed hotel info and photos
4. Books room with guest details
5. After check-in: Orders room service via app
6. Tracks order status in real-time
7. Uses waiter call for requests

**Key Features Used**:
- Hotel search and filtering
- Booking system
- Room service ordering
- Order tracking
- Waiter call
- Profile management

## 👨‍🍳 Persona 4: Kitchen Chief

### **Meet Kumar - Head Chef**

**Demographics**:
- Age: 45
- Location: Chitwan, Nepal
- Experience: 20 years in kitchen
- Team: 5 cooks
- Tech Savviness: Low-Moderate

**Goals**:
- ✅ Receive orders clearly and instantly
- ✅ Prioritize orders efficiently
- ✅ Update order status in real-time
- ✅ Reduce order errors and remakes
- ✅ Manage kitchen workflow

**Pain Points**:
- ❌ Handwritten order slips are illegible
- ❌ Orders get lost or delayed
- ❌ Can't prioritize urgent orders
- ❌ Waiters keep asking "Is it ready?"
- ❌ No visibility into pending orders

**How StayHaven Helps**:
- Digital KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket)
- Clear order details with notes
- Priority indicators (normal/high)
- One-click status updates
- Real-time order queue

**User Journey**:
1. Logs in to kitchen dashboard
2. Sees all pending orders in queue
3. Clicks "Preparing" when starting
4. Cooks the dish
5. Clicks "Ready" when complete
6. Waiter gets instant notification
7. Order automatically moves to delivered

**Key Features Used**:
- Kitchen dashboard
- Order queue view
- Status update buttons
- Priority indicators
- Order history

## 🍽️ Persona 5: Waiter

### **Meet Sita - Restaurant Waiter**

**Demographics**:
- Age: 24
- Location: Kathmandu, Nepal
- Experience: 3 years
- Works: Breakfast, lunch, dinner shifts
- Tech Savviness: Moderate

**Goals**:
- ✅ Take orders quickly and accurately
- ✅ Know when orders are ready
- ✅ Respond to guest calls instantly
- ✅ Manage multiple tables efficiently
- ✅ Provide excellent service

**Pain Points**:
- ❌ Forgets table orders (paper slips)
- ❌ Runs to kitchen to check status
- ❌ Misses guest calls (busy with other tables)
- ❌ Order mistakes lead to remakes
- ❌ Hard to track which tables need attention

**How StayHaven Helps**:
- Mobile-friendly order taking
- Real-time order status notifications
- Instant waiter call alerts
- Table/room assignment tracking
- Order history per table

**User Journey**:
1. Logs in to waiter dashboard
2. Sees assigned tables/rooms
3. Takes guest order via app
4. Submits to kitchen (KOT generated)
5. Gets notification when order is ready
6. Delivers food to guest
7. Marks order as delivered
8. Receives instant alert for new waiter calls

**Key Features Used**:
- Waiter dashboard
- Order creation
- Real-time notifications
- Waiter call alerts
- Table assignments
- Order status tracking
