# Problem Statement

> Identifying the market gaps and challenges that StayHaven addresses

---

## 🎯 The Core Problem

### Traditional Hotel Booking Platforms Are Disconnected from Operations

Most hotel booking platforms (Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb) focus **only** on the booking experience. Once a guest checks in, hotels must use **separate systems** for:
- Room service orders
- Staff management
- Kitchen operations
- Guest requests
- Inventory tracking

This creates **operational silos** and **inefficiency**.

---

## 🔍 Market Research Findings

### Problem 1: Fragmented Systems
**Challenge**: Hotels use 5-10 different software systems
```
Booking Platform (3rd party)
  ↓
Property Management System (PMS)
  ↓
Point of Sale (POS) for restaurant
  ↓
Room Service App (separate)
  ↓
Staff Scheduling Tool
  ↓
Guest Communication Platform
```

**Impact**:
- ❌ Data doesn't sync between systems
- ❌ Staff juggle multiple apps
- ❌ Higher operational costs (multiple subscriptions)
- ❌ Poor guest experience (disconnected services)

---

### Problem 2: No Real-Time Order Management
**Challenge**: Paper-based or slow digital order systems

**Current Reality in Most Hotels**:
1. Guest calls reception for room service
2. Receptionist writes order on paper
3. Runner takes paper to kitchen
4. Kitchen prepares (no status tracking)
5. Waiter delivers (guest doesn't know when)

**Impact**:
- ❌ Orders get lost or delayed
- ❌ No visibility into order status
- ❌ Kitchen can't prioritize efficiently
- ❌ Guest frustration (no ETAs)

---

### Problem 3: Lack of Multi-Property Management
**Challenge**: Hotel chains can't manage multiple properties efficiently

**Scenario**: A hotel group owns 5 properties
- Each property uses different systems
- Owner can't see consolidated analytics
- Staff can't transfer between properties easily
- No centralized control or branding

**Impact**:
- ❌ Operational complexity
- ❌ Inconsistent guest experience
- ❌ Difficult to scale
- ❌ Higher training costs

---

### Problem 4: Poor Staff Communication
**Challenge**: Manual, slow communication between staff roles

**Example**: Guest needs housekeeping
1. Guest calls reception
2. Reception notes it down
3. Tries to call housekeeping (may miss call)
4. Housekeeping eventually responds
5. No tracking of resolution time

**Impact**:
- ❌ Slow service response
- ❌ Missed requests
- ❌ No accountability
- ❌ Poor guest satisfaction scores

---

### Problem 5: High Commission Fees
**Challenge**: Third-party booking platforms charge 15-25% commission

**Example**: $100 room booking
- OTA Commission: $20-25
- Hotel keeps: $75-80

**Impact**:
- ❌ Reduced profit margins
- ❌ Hotels increase prices to offset fees
- ❌ Guests pay more
- ❌ Hotels have no direct relationship with guests

---
